# Task: character sheet for an animated motion-graphics ad (stage 1 of 2)

Use your built-in image generation tool. Do not draw with code. Copy each generated image into this working directory with the exact file name below (PNG). Then write `prompts.md` here with the exact final prompt and size for each file. Print the file list with pixel sizes at the end.

## Context
A 95-second Korean motion-graphics ad for a student concert-ticketing demo service. The main character, HARIN, will be animated later with squash-and-stretch and snappy, bouncy motion graphics, so she must be drawn as a clean flat 2D character that is easy to cut into parts and animate.

## Character: HARIN
- Korean woman, early 20s, university student. Expressive face; she shows feelings more with her face and body than with words.
- Shoulder-length straight dark-brown bob hair with bangs. Large expressive eyes, simple nose, small mouth.
- Outfit: oversized hot-pink (#FF2D78) hoodie, light-blue jeans, white sneakers. She often holds a smartphone. Simple, memorable silhouette.
- She is a fan of a fictional idol group; nothing on her clothes refers to any real group, logo or text.

## Style (most important)
- Clean, modern 2D vector-like character illustration, like a polished motion-graphics explainer or a Korean webtoon cover: smooth confident outlines in dark ink (#14101A), flat cel shading with at most one shadow tone per color, crisp anti-aliased edges.
- ABSOLUTELY NO grain, noise, film grain, paper texture, halftone dots, crosshatching, scratchy or sketchy lines, jagged, pixelated or dithered edges, watercolor bleeding, or speckles. Every color area must be perfectly flat and clean, as if it were an SVG.
- Palette accents: hot pink #FF2D78, sunflower yellow #FFD23F, cobalt blue #2B3BE5, warm paper #FFF8EE, ink #14101A.
- No text, letters, logos, watermarks or UI anywhere in the image.
